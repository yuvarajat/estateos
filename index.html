An everyday example

One enquiry.
A few thoughtful steps.

A buyer calls about a two-bedroom home. Here’s how the team can keep the conversation moving without losing the details.

Show me this in a demo →
  1. 01

    “We’re looking for a 2 BHK.”

    Record their preferred area, budget and timeline. Assign someone to take the conversation forward.

  2. 02

    “Can we see it this weekend?”

    Check the available properties and schedule an appointment. Keep the buyer’s preferences with their record.

  3. 03

    “Please send us the details.”

    Prepare a quotation and record the next follow-up, so the colleague helping tomorrow has the same context.

  4. 04

    “What happens after we book?”

    Track the booking and post-booking milestones. Give the customer a place to follow their journey.
