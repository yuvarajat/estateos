Ask us something else ↗
We already use Excel and WhatsApp. Where does this fit?

You can use EstateOS for shared property records, lead ownership and follow-ups, so those details are easier to find. In a demo, bring an example of how your team works today and we can walk through it together.

Can we try it with a small team?

The Pilot plan is a free 30-day trial for one company admin and one employee, with up to 100 properties. Use it to see how the core workflows fit your day before choosing a paid plan.

Does every plan include everything on this page?

No. Access depends on the plan and each person’s permissions. Messaging providers, integrations and AI enhancements also need configuration. Ask us to confirm the features you need before you choose a plan.

What happens when I request a demo?

The form prepares a WhatsApp message with your contact details and selected plan. You review and send it in WhatsApp, then speak with the Yuvathy team about what you’d like to see.
