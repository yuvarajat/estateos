Cookies & analytics

Here’s what runs on this marketing website and where you can control your browser’s storage.

Last updated: 22 October 2026
On this pageWebsite analyticsBrowser storageYour choicesExternal servicesAsk a question

1. Website analytics

This marketing website loads Google Analytics using the Google tag. It helps us understand visits and how people use the site. Google Analytics can use cookies and collect information about page visits, browser and device characteristics, and interactions.

The current website does not offer an on-site cookie preference panel. You can manage cookies and tracking through your browser settings.

2. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small pieces of information a website or service can store in your browser. The EstateOS marketing page does not require you to sign in. The EstateOS app is a separate service; its login and account storage are covered by the Privacy Policy.

The website’s font is served from this website, rather than requested from Google Fonts.

3. Your browser controls

You can inspect, block or clear cookies in your browser’s privacy and site-data settings. Your browser may also offer tracking protection or controls for third-party cookies. These settings apply according to the browser you use.

Clearing cookies does not remove information already received by an analytics provider. For questions about your personal information, see the choices and contact details in our Privacy Policy.

4. Links to other services

If you use a WhatsApp contact link or submit the demo form, WhatsApp opens with a prepared message for you to review and send. Visiting WhatsApp, the EstateOS app or another linked website takes you to that service, which has its own privacy practices and browser storage.

5. Ask us a question

Contact Yuvathy Technologies at hello@yuvathy.in if you have a question about cookies or analytics on this website.
